## 8. Correction — duplicate clustering (added during Phase 2 preflight)

The table in §5 reports the **raw** duplicate-clustering output and is retained
unchanged as a Phase 1 artefact. Its "groups spanning different labels" figure
(**42 groups / 866 files**) must not be interpreted as label noise.

`src/data/cluster_diagnostics.py` showed that the union-find clustering computes a
transitive closure, which chained unrelated images together: at the threshold used,
**one component holds 732 images (11.9% of the corpus), spans all four vegetables,
and has a diameter of 39 against a threshold of 6.** A genuine duplicate burst
cannot have a diameter six times its own threshold.

Corrected interpretation:

| Kind | Groups | Images |
|---|---:|---:|
| Raw threshold-6 output | 42 | 866 |
| Chained artefact (one component) | 1 | 732 |
| Genuine label ambiguity | 41 | 134 |

The split was not rebuilt. Over-grouping is conservative for leakage but places the
732-image block entirely in train; see risk R1 in `reports/phase2_preflight.md`.

Evidence: `evaluation/reports/cluster_diagnostics.json`.
